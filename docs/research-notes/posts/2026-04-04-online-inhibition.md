- Dissociative Anonymity: Users feel that because they cannot be easily identified, their behavior doesn't matter.
- Invisibility: Not being seen or seeing others (e.g., in text-based chat) removes nonverbal cues, lowering inhibitions.
- Asynchronicity: Interactions do not happen in real-time, allowing users to communicate at their own pace without immediate social consequences.
